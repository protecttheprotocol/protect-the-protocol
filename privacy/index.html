Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Protect the Protocol! ("we," "our," or "the project") is a grassroots community initiative advocating for open, decentralized social infrastructure. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit protecttheprotocol.org, interact with its campaign features, or play its browser-based game.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is designed to operate without requiring visitors to create accounts, provide personal information, connect cryptocurrency wallets, or register to participate.

We do not currently operate an independent analytics system, advertising tracker, user registration system, or database of website visitors.

However, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps, and requested resources to deliver their services, maintain security, and operate their infrastructure.

2. Campaign Ballot

The YES/NO ballot on our homepage is an interactive expression of the campaign's message. It is not an official election, referendum, or verified public opinion survey.

Currently, your selection is handled locally in your browser. We do not transmit, collect, permanently store, or count your selection on a central server. The ballot receipt is generated within the page and does not serve as proof of a recorded vote.

If a public vote-counting feature is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the information collected and how votes are processed.

3. Browser-Based NES Game

Our /play/ page uses EmulatorJS, a third-party browser-based emulator, to run the Protect the Protocol NES game.

The emulator operates on your device after its required files are loaded. It may use browser storage, including local storage, caches, or IndexedDB, to maintain preferences, control configurations, saved games, and related emulator data.

Game saves, screenshots, or recordings that you choose to download may be stored on your device. We do not currently operate a server-side system for collecting player progress, scores, or gameplay identities.

Emulator files are loaded from cdn.emulatorjs.org. Connecting to this service may disclose technical request information to the CDN provider.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our custom campaign code does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. The embedded emulator may use browser storage for game-related functionality. Hosting and third-party providers may use technologies governed by their own policies.

You can manage or delete locally stored website data through your browser settings. Clearing site data may also remove saved game information or emulator preferences. Files you downloaded separately must be deleted from your device if you no longer want them.

5. Videos and Downloads

Our campaign video is delivered as a website resource. Playback controls, including pause and sound settings, are managed in your browser. We do not currently operate a system that associates video viewing behavior with individual visitor profiles.

Downloading a campaign sticker or other available materials transfers a copy to your device. The project does not require you to provide personal information to download these materials.

6. External Websites

Our website contains links to external services, including Devcon and X. If you choose to share campaign content through X or follow an external link, the destination service may process information under its own privacy policy.

We do not control the privacy or security practices of external services.

7. Hosting and Security

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to serve the website and protect their systems. Their collection, retention, and security practices are governed by their applicable policies.

We take reasonable care in maintaining the website but cannot guarantee that any website or third-party service is completely secure.

8. Retention and Your Choices

Because the current site does not operate user accounts or a visitor database, we generally do not maintain individual visitor records that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted through us.

Information processed by hosting providers or external services is subject to their own retention practices. You may use your browser controls to clear locally stored data or manage relevant privacy permissions.

Where applicable law provides additional privacy rights, you may contact us to ask about those rights and our processing of your information.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children through the current website features. If you believe personal information relating to a child has been collected by us, please contact the project.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the project evolves, including if we introduce vote counting, community accounts, additional services, or new data processing features. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Protect the Protocol project maintainers at: dev@protecttheprotocol.org.
